---
name: Blog republishing
description: Why admin tip copying preserves rich text and provides separate image-copy and download alternatives.
---

The creator reuses 꿀Tip articles in external blogs and wants to copy both content and images from 꿀팁 관리.

**Why:** Copying a link or raw HTML alone does not satisfy this workflow. Blog editors differ in whether they accept remotely referenced images in an HTML clipboard payload.

**How to apply:** Preserve formatted article text and relevant images without changing the original post. Use verified published absolute URLs, not preview addresses, in exported HTML. Keep separate binary image-copy and image-file download alternatives; do not promise that every blog editor will retain HTML images.
