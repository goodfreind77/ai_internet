---
name: Landing consultation privacy
description: Why landing-page inquiries remain in the site's consultation inbox rather than external notifications.
---

Landing-page consultation submissions should stay in the site's protected consultation inbox by default, without forwarding customer contact details to a third-party messaging service.

**Why:** The page's explicit consent covers collecting contact details for consultation, not sending those details to an external notification provider. Reusing the older consultation notification behavior would expand disclosure without appropriate notice.

**How to apply:** When adding notifications for landing inquiries, first review the privacy notice and consent with the business owner; consider a notification without personally identifiable details.
