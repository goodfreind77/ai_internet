Tip covers should match the title and substance of each individual article, not merely its broad category.

**Why:** The creator rejected a small set of shared category photographs as insufficiently related to the articles and confirmed separate images for every article.

**How to apply:** Use article-specific visual concepts when replacing or extending the gallery. Do not simplify existing article covers back into a few shared category images; category artwork is only a fallback for posts without their own cover.
