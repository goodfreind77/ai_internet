For view-count requests, identify the referenced article by its date or title before choosing the content section and batch scope.

**Why:** The creator clarified that the intended target was "최근에 올린 꿀팁 조회수" after an adjustment to customer reviews missed the requested articles. Initially treating the complaint only as a missing publication also missed the scope error.

**How to apply:** When section labels and specific post references disagree, use the clarified article reference or ask once. Verify the referenced post's current public data, and distinguish counter changes from publication-date changes. Also distinguish filling remaining zero counters from adding to every counter; do not repeat an increase on already-filled posts when the request concerns remaining zeros.
